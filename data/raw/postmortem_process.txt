Postmortem Process (plain-text reference copy)

Every Sev-1 and Sev-2 incident requires a written postmortem within 5 business days of
resolution. The postmortem document uses the standard template: timeline, impact (customers
affected, duration, revenue impact if known), root cause, what went well, what went poorly, and
action items.

Postmortems are blameless. The document must describe what happened and why the system and
process allowed it to happen, not focus on individual mistakes. Language like "X should have
known" is flagged in review and rewritten.

Action items from a postmortem are filed as tickets with a named owner and a due date, and are
tracked to completion in the weekly Reliability sync. A postmortem with no action items is treated
as incomplete and sent back for revision, unless the incident writeup explicitly justifies why no
follow-up work is needed.

Postmortems for Sev-1 incidents are presented verbally in the weekly Reliability sync by the
Incident Commander. Sev-2 postmortems are shared async in the #reliability channel and reviewed
only if a reader flags a concern within 3 days.
